FAQ

How does a LinkedIn text formatter work?

LinkedIn has no native bold or italic. This tool swaps normal letters for look-alike Unicode characters, so the styling survives copy and paste.

Does Unicode bold hurt accessibility or search?

Screen readers can read styled characters poorly and LinkedIn search does not index them as normal words. Style short hooks and headings only, and keep keywords in plain text.

Where does LinkedIn cut off a post?

Around the first 3 lines (about 140 characters on mobile, 210 on desktop). The preview approximates this; LinkedIn can change it.
